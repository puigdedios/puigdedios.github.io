Research

Yunied Puig de Dios

I work in functional analysis and operator theory, especially on the dynamics of linear operators. More recently, my research examines how infinite-dimensional operator dynamics can shape the theoretical foundations of deep learning, particularly structured representation learning.

New York, NY puigdedios@gmail.com

Research Interests

! theoretical machine learning, deep learning theory, linear dynamics, operator-theoretic methods in ML, interpretability, generalization, structured learning.

About

I am a mathematician and deep learning researcher with a Ph.D. in Pure Mathematics, specializing in functional analysis, operator theory, and linear dynamics. My research bridges infinite-dimensional operator theory with the theoretical foundations of deep learning.

Research

My work lies at the intersection of functional analysis, operator theory, linear dynamics, and theoretical machine learning. I am particularly interested in operator-theoretic methods in machine learning, interpretability, generalization, and structured learning.
